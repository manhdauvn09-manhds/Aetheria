✨ Aetheria — Game Features (Detailed)

Deep-dive into every system, mechanic, and progression path.

1. Combat System (Turn-Based Grid Battle)

Battlefield Layout

  • 6×8 Hex Grid: Asymmetrical battlefield with varied elevation.
  • Elevation: High ground grants +10% defense; low ground applies -10% defense. Melee vs ranged treated differently.
  • Elemental Tiles: Verdant → mushroom patches (heal 5% HP/turn). Ember → lava (burn on step). Frost → icy terrain (slow). Tide → water (drown risk). Sky → wind shear (knockback). Void → reality rifts (chaotic damage).
  • Hazards: Traps, lava flows, ice walls. Some are destructible.

Action Point (AP) System

Core Mechanic: Each hero gets 3 AP per turn.
  • Move 1 hex: 1 AP
  • Basic attack: 1 AP
  • Skill (varies): 1–2 AP
  • Defend (parry): Free (next turn)
  • Bank AP: Unspent AP carries over, capped at 1 per turn (max 4 total)

Resonance (Combo System)

Trigger: When two heroes chain consecutive turns with matching elements (e.g., Lyra uses Frost skill, then Kyo uses Frost skill).
  • Effect: +50% damage to both attacks; AoE burst at impact location.
  • Build Meter: Each status effect or element match adds 1 stack (max 5). At 5 stacks, Aether-Surge grants free skill next turn.
  • Timing: Must activate within 2 consecutive turns.

2. Progression System

Account Level (1–100)

  • XP Curve: Exponential. Lv 1 = 100 XP, Lv 50 = 10,000 XP, Lv 100 = 50,000 XP.
  • Milestone Unlocks:
    • Lv 5: Inventory expanded (+20 slots)
    • Lv 15: Photo Mode unlocked
    • Lv 25: Co-op raids unlocked
    • Lv 40: PvP ranked mode starts (Bronze tier)
    • Lv 50: Skill tree respec becomes free (was 1 gold)
    • Lv 75: Mythic-tier loot drops begin
    • Lv 90: Endless Tower unlocked
    • Lv 100: Convergence (final story act); multiple endings

Character Ascension (A0–A3)

Per-character tiers. Each tier costs:
  • A0 → A1: 5 Aether-cores (common), 500 gold
  • A1 → A2: 10 Aether-cores (rare), 2,000 gold, 1 duplicate
  • A2 → A3: 20 Aether-cores (epic), 5,000 gold, 2 duplicates, 1 Mythic shard
Stat boost per tier: +10% HP, +5% ATK, +3% DEF, +2% SPD

Skill Tree (12 Nodes per Hero)

  • Structure: 3 branches (offense, defense, utility). Each branch has 4 nodes in a linear path.
  • Node Types:
    • Stat nodes: +ATK, +DEF, +HP, +SPD
    • Skill unlock nodes: New active or passive ability
    • Synergy nodes: Bonus if paired with certain teammates
  • Respec: Free at towns after Lv 50 (otherwise 1 gold per point).

Crafting (Forge System)

Recipes:
  • Merge (3 duplicates + shard): Upgrade 1 tier (Common → Rare)
  • Ascend (5 Aether-cores): Boost stats without changing tier
  • Transmute (1 Epic + 1 Mythic blueprint): Craft new Mythic item

3. Exploration & Story (6 Realms)

Realm Mechanics

  • Overworld Graph: Each realm is a node map. Nodes are: Story, Combat, Puzzle, Treasure, Boss, Hidden.
  • Discovery Meter: Explores environmental secrets. 100% unlocks: secret boss, cosmetic reward, lore codex.
  • Narrative Twist: At 50% realm completion, major story reveal (e.g., betrayal, hidden ally).
  • Dynamic Events: Weather/time-of-day changes spawn rare encounters (e.g., storm = rare elemental boss).
  • Photo Mode: Capture scenic vistas, share on social feed. Engagement rewards apply.

4. Multiplayer Features

PvP (Ranked System)

Ranking: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Mythic (6 tiers)
  • Matchmaking: Glicko-2 MMR. Queue time < 30s at peak.
  • Reward Structure: Win = +20 MMR (Diamond+), +10 (others). Loss = -5 MMR (soft floor at 0).
  • Seasonal Reset: Monthly. Top 1% earn exclusive cosmetic.
  • Anti-cheat: Server-authoritative validation. Replay hash verification detects tampering.

Guilds

  • Capacity: 30 members max.
  • Roles: Leader, Officer, Member. Permissions for treasury, invites, kick.
  • Weekly Raids: 3-player synchronous raid boss. Exclusive loot (Epic+ items).
  • Guild Storage: 100 item slots; members can deposit/withdraw.
  • Guild Leaderboard: Ranked by weekly raid completion speed.

Co-op (3-Player Raids)

  • Realtime Synchronous: All 3 heroes' turns resolve simultaneously (not turn-by-turn).
  • Communication: Voice chat or text callouts in-raid.
  • Boss Mechanics: Team coordination required (e.g., 2 must attack simultaneously to stagger boss).
  • Loot Share: Drops go to guild treasury; fairness via rolling.

Leaderboards

  • Global: Top 100 highest-level players (account level).
  • Regional: By server drift (latency-optimized region).
  • Guild: Sorted by member count and weekly raid completion.
  • Friends: Private leaderboard within your friend group.
  • PvP: Ranked by MMR (Mythic tier only, live-updated every 5 min).

5. Social & Chat

  • Global Chat: Server-wide messages. Profanity filter; spam bans (3 strikes).
  • Guild Chat: Members only. Moderator pins; message history 30 days.
  • Party Chat: In-raid or co-op team. Private.
  • Whisper: 1-on-1 private message. Offline messages queued (max 50).
  • Report System: Flag toxic players. Game moderators review within 24 hours; bans up to permanent for severe cases.

6. Marketplace & Economy

  • Soft Currency (Gold): In-game earnable. Use for: gear upgrades, skill respec, Forge recipes, marketplace.
  • Premium Currency (Gems): Real-money only. Use for: battle pass, cosmetic skins, founder pack.
  • Marketplace Listings: Gear, crafting materials. No stats-affecting items (cosmetics only for premium).
  • Price Dynamics: Algorithmic pricing prevents inflation/deflation. Rare items auto-adjust based on supply.
  • No Loot Boxes: All cosmetics visible before purchase.

7. Daily & Seasonal Content

Daily Login

  • Day 1: 100 gold
  • Day 3: 1 Rare item
  • Day 7: 500 gold + 5 Aether-cores (streak reset, 2x multiplier for next cycle)

Weekly Quests

  • Defeat 5 bosses
  • Win 3 PvP matches
  • Complete 1 guild raid
  • Explore 50% of a realm
  • Rewards: 1,000 gold + 1 Epic loot box

Seasonal Pass (Free + Premium)

  • Free Track: 30 tiers. Rewards: cosmetics (weapon skins, emotes), gold, Aether-cores.
  • Premium Track: 40 additional tiers. Exclusive skins and effects (no stat advantage).
  • Duration: 60 days per season. Auto-progress via gameplay; no grind gates.

Endless Tower (Roguelike)

  • Procedural: 50 floors. Each floor has random enemy composition and modifier.
  • Modifiers: "Weak heroes heal 50% less," "enemies gain +2 AP," etc.
  • Rewards: Scaling by floor cleared. Floor 1 = 50 gold, Floor 50 = 10,000 gold + Mythic shard.
  • Daily Reset: 1 free run per day; can purchase more (100 gems each).

8. Save & Sync System

Auto-save Triggers: Turn end, node enter/exit, menu open, idle 10s.
  • Cloud Sync: IndexedDB cache (local) + delta sync to server every 30s.
  • Offline Mode: Play offline for up to 1 hour; changes auto-merge on reconnect.
  • Resume Banner: "Continue Lv 24 — Frostspire Bridge (8 min ago)"
  • Manual Slots: 3 save slots for experiment/branching playstyles.
