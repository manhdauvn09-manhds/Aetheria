⚔️ Aetheria — Player Guide

Master the broken sky. Reclaim the Shards. Become Aetherbound.

1. Getting Started

Signing Up

Create your account via email, Google, or Discord. Verify your email to unlock multiplayer features (PvP, guilds, leaderboards).

Your First Steps

  • Meet Aevra (your hero) in the Verdant Reach, the starting realm.
  • Learn combat — turn-based battles on hex grids. Every turn you get 3 Action Points (AP).
  • Discover secrets — hidden treasures, lore fragments, and rare items hide in every corner.
  • Auto-save is always on — you can resume exactly where you left off.

2. Core Gameplay

Combat System

ConceptExplanation
Grid Combat6×8 hex map with elevation, elements, and hazards.
Action Points (AP)3 per turn. Move, attack, use skills—each costs AP.
ElementsVerdant (nature), Ember (fire), Frost (ice), Tide (water), Sky (lightning), Void (chaos).
ResonanceChain two heroes with matching elements for +50% damage AoE combo.
Status EffectsBurn, Freeze, Poison, Stagger, Aether-Surge (builds combos).
💡 Tip: Smart positioning beats raw stats. Use terrain and status effects to control the battlefield.

Progression

  • Account Level: 1–100 (unlocks new mechanics every 5 levels).
  • Character Ascension: A0–A3 tiers; boost stats via Aether-cores and gear.
  • Skill Tree: Each character has 12 talent nodes. Respec anytime at town.
  • Loot Tiers: Common → Rare → Epic → Mythic → Aether-Forged.
  • Crafting: Combine duplicates and shards to upgrade gear at the Forge.

4. Characters (Your Party)

Start with Aevra. Recruit more heroes via story quests and special events:

  • Kyo (Bladepriest): Tank/DPS with parry chains. Unlock at Lv 11.
  • Lyra (Stormcaller): AoE mage. Unlock at Lv 18.
  • Brann (Earthwarden): Tank specialist. Unlock at Lv 25.
  • Mira (Lifebinder): Healer. Unlock at Lv 32.
  • Vex (Shadowblade): Assassin. Unlock at PvP rank D.
  • Solen (Sunoracle): Support. Unlock via raid.
  • Null (Voidchild): Wildcard. Secret unlock at Lv 80.

5. Online & Social

Multiplayer Features

  • Leaderboards: Global, regional, guild, friends rankings.
  • PvP (Ranked): Bronze → Mythic tiers. Season resets monthly. Queue time < 30s at peak.
  • Guilds: Up to 30 members. Weekly raids with exclusive rewards.
  • Co-op Raids: 3-player realtime raids. Teamwork required.
  • Chat: Global, party, guild, whisper. Profanity-filtered; report toxic players.
  • Marketplace: Buy/sell with soft currency (gold). No pay-to-win.

Daily & Seasonal Content

  • Daily Quests: 3 per day. Complete for streak bonuses.
  • Weekly Quests: Tougher challenges, better rewards.
  • Seasonal Pass: Free + premium tracks. Cosmetics only (no stat advantage).
  • Endless Tower: Procedural roguelike. Climb for daily challenges.

6. Saving & Resuming

🎮 Cloud Save: Every action is auto-saved. Go offline, resume anytime. Your progress syncs automatically.
  • Resume Banner: On login, see your last position (e.g., "Continue Lv 24 — Frostspire Bridge, 8 min ago").
  • Manual Slots: 3 save slots for branching playstyles.
  • Conflict Resolution: If you edit offline, local changes merge with server state on reconnect.

7. Shop & Monetization

  • Founders Pack: One-time premium bundle (cosmetics + starter items).
  • Battle Pass: Seasonal cosmetics. No stat advantage.
  • Marketplace: Trade gear for soft currency (gold). No loot boxes.

8. Tips & Tricks

✨ Master Tips:
  • Use Photo Mode at vistas — share for social engagement rewards.
  • Combine elements for Resonance combos — 50% damage boost is game-changing.
  • Complete side quests for rare items before tackling main story.
  • Check the Codex for lore — every character and realm has hidden stories.
  • Join a guild early — weekly raids drop exclusive gear.

9. Glossary

  • Aether: The world's life-energy; shattered into 7 Shards after the Cataclysm.
  • Aetherbound: Heroes attuned to an Aether Shard.
  • Resonance: Combo trigger when two party members chain matching elements.
  • Ascension: Character tier upgrades (A0 → A3) via Aether-cores.
  • Drift: Server region (global, regional, or latency-optimized).

Account & Privacy (v1.1)

  • Login attempts are rate-limited (10/min per IP). If you mistype your password too many times, wait one minute and retry.
  • Sessions: Your access token lives only in memory; closing the tab logs you out of that session. The "remember me" hint stored in your browser keeps your name/avatar for the next visit but never your password or email.
  • Two-factor: Sign in with Google or Discord for OAuth-grade protection (recommended).
  • Password reset: Use the Forgot password? link on the login page. The reset email expires in 30 minutes — request a new one if you miss the window.
  • Cheating policy: Combat replays are server-verified via a deterministic hash. Tampered runs are auto-flagged and the account is suspended pending review. Appeals: support@aetheria.com.
